# Privacy

Last reviewed: 2026-08-20

Navnoor Research is a public static site. It has no login, analytics, telemetry,
advertising, third-party script, remote font, cookie, service worker, or backend
receiving user input.

Search terms, selected filters, and opened records remain in page memory. They
are not written to the URL, browser storage, clipboard, or a network request.
Following a source link leaves Navnoor Research and is then governed by that
publisher's privacy policy.

Do not enter confidential research, personal information, holdings, orders, or
other sensitive information. The product intentionally provides no field for
storing them.
